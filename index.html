About Our Breed

Dachshunds are small dogs with big personalities! Known for their distinctive long bodies and short legs, these charming companions are loyal, playful, and full of character. At Veltrique Dachshuns, we specialize in breeding healthy, well-tempered dachshunds that make perfect family pets.
