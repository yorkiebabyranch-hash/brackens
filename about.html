About Bracken Doxies

Our Story

Bracken Doxies was founded with a passion for breeding healthy, happy dachshunds. What started as a personal love for the breed has grown into a dedicated breeding operation focused on producing exceptional puppies with ideal temperaments and physical characteristics.

Our name, "Bracken," reflects the beautiful fern-like texture we see in nature—symbolizing growth, vitality, and natural beauty, much like our puppies!

Our Mission

At Bracken Doxies, our mission is to:

🎯 Breed with Purpose

We carefully select breeding pairs that exemplify the best qualities of the dachshund breed, ensuring healthy genetics and wonderful personalities.

❤️ Prioritize Puppy Welfare

Every puppy receives individualized attention, early socialization, and comprehensive health care from birth until they join their forever families.

🤝 Build Lasting Relationships

We don't just sell puppies—we build lasting relationships with our puppy families and provide support throughout the dog's life.

Our Journey

2009: Fell in love with the dachshund breed and brought our first dog home
2011: Began formal breeding program with extensive research and health certifications
2015: Established Bracken Doxies as a registered breeding operation
2018: Expanded breeding program while maintaining commitment to quality over quantity
2026: Celebrating years of happy families and healthy dachshunds across the country

Our Commitment to Health

We conduct comprehensive health screenings on all our breeding dogs, including genetic testing and veterinary evaluations. Every puppy receives:

  • Initial vaccinations and microchipping
  • Veterinary health certificate
  • Health guarantee (2 years)
  • Lifetime breeder support
  • Deworming and flea prevention

Why We're Different

Unlike large-scale breeding operations, Bracken Doxies operates on a small, intimate scale. We raise our puppies in our home, not in kennels, ensuring they're exposed to normal household sounds, activities, and socialization from day one. This makes them well-adjusted, confident companions ready for their new families.
