💡 What's Included with Your Puppy?

  • Initial Vaccinations: Up-to-date on all age-appropriate vaccines
  • Microchip: Registered with your information for safety
  • Veterinary Health Certificate: Issued by our trusted veterinarian
  • 2-Year Health Guarantee: Protection against genetic health issues
  • Starter Kit: Sample of food, treats, and toys
  • Lifetime Support: We're here for any questions or concerns
  • AKC Registration: Eligible for registration (papers available)

🐾 Our Adoption Process

Step 1: Browse available puppies and contact us

Step 2: Complete our adoption application

Step 3: Phone consultation to discuss your lifestyle and preferences

Step 4: Sign adoption contract and health agreement

Step 5: Make deposit to reserve your puppy

Step 6: Pickup or arrange transportation at 8-12 weeks old
