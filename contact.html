Frequently Asked Questions

How much do puppies cost?
Pricing varies based on the puppy's pedigree, color, coat type, and health certifications. Please contact us for current pricing information for available puppies.
Do you have a waiting list?
Yes, we maintain a waiting list for upcoming litters. Interested families are encouraged to apply and make a deposit to secure a spot on the list.
What's included in the health guarantee?
Our 2-year health guarantee covers genetic health issues. It includes veterinary examination, vaccinations, microchipping, and ongoing breeder support for the puppy's lifetime.
Can I visit before purchasing?
Absolutely! We encourage potential families to visit our home and meet our puppies and parents before making a commitment. Please contact us to schedule a visit.
Do you offer payment plans?
We work with families to find payment arrangements that work for them. Please discuss payment options when you contact us about available puppies.
What if my circumstances change?
We have a return policy for puppies adopted from our program. No questions asked—we believe our dogs deserve loving homes, and we'll help find the right solution.
Are you AKC registered?
Our breeding dogs are AKC registered, and puppies are eligible for registration. AKC papers are available for an additional fee.
Do you breed long-haired or miniature dachshunds?
Currently, we specialize in standard wirehaired dachshunds. Please reach out to discuss your preferences—we may be able to refer you to reputable breeders if we don't have what you're looking for.

We Look Forward to Hearing From You!

Whether you're inquiring about available puppies, have questions about our breeding program, or just want to chat about dachshunds, we'd love to connect with you. Your new family member might be waiting!
